Quick guides

Small checks that save re-shoots

Short-form platforms recompose, crop and cover your video in different ways. A few pre-export habits make your captions and subjects survive that process.

Frame for 9:16 first

A 1080 × 1920 canvas is the common vertical reference. If you edit in a different ratio, export a true vertical copy rather than relying on the platform to crop a landscape video.

Protect the middle band

Profile names, captions, like counts and buttons usually sit near the top, bottom and right edge. Keep faces, product shots and burned-in text away from those bands so nothing important gets hidden.

Read subtitles out loud

Around 17 characters per second is a useful ceiling for quick captions. Two short lines are easier to scan than one long line, and a cue that appears for less than a second is easy to miss.

Check the file, not just the idea

Resolution, duration and size all affect how smoothly a clip uploads and plays. Catching an accidental 4K, three-minute export before you publish is much cheaper than re-uploading later.
